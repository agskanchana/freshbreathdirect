Inside a CoolCaps pearl

Four layers in one seamless capsule, built to work in two places. Choose a layer to see what it does.

Why seamless? The shell and filling form together in a single drop, so there's no join to leak or crack. It's the same Japanese capsule technology used for delicate liquids, made small enough to sit on your tongue.

Two phases, one pearl

  1. In the first few seconds

    Fresh mouth

    The outer pearl dissolves and releases strong mint, neutralising odour where you notice it first.

  2. Once you swallow

    Fresh from within

    The inner pearl opens in your stomach and keeps working, which is why the effect lasts beyond the mint taste.

Lab-tested against bad‑breath compounds

Hydrogen sulphide and methyl mercaptan are two of the sulphur compounds behind bad breath. In the manufacturer's laboratory test, the CoolCaps breathcare flavour cut both, and the effect on methyl mercaptan grew over 30 minutes.

Manufacturer's GC/FPD laboratory test on a standard odour solution, compared with the same solution plus water. A lab measurement, not a clinical trial. Results in people will vary.

Questions

How do I take a CoolCaps pearl?

Put one pearl on your tongue and let the outer shell melt. Once you taste the mint, swallow the small inner pearl. You can take one or more as you need them.

Pearls, strips or spray: which should I choose?

Pearls work in your mouth and stomach, so they last longest. Strips melt in seconds and are the most discreet. The spray gives a quick top-up and holds 200 sprays.

Are they keto friendly?

Yes. They are sugar free, and a CoolCaps pearl has under half a calorie and about 0.01 g of carbohydrate.

Will they break a fast?

Each pearl is 0.48 kcal. Whether that matters depends on how strict your fast is.

Do you deliver across the UK?

Yes. Orders are packed in Huntingdon, Cambridgeshire, and sent across the UK.
